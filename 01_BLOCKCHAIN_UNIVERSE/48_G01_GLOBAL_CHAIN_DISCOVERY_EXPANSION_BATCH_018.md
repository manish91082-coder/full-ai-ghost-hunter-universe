- A discovery name is not yet a canonical execution identity.
- Aliases, rebrands and ecosystem labels remain unresolved until primary identity verification.
- EVM chain IDs are not inferred from names.
- Non-EVM networks retain their native identity model.
- L2/L3/appchain labels are not merged merely because they share a stack.

- Testnet/devnet evidence is excluded from the production candidate denominator.
- Duplicate identities discovered through multiple sources are retained as provenance, not counted twice.
