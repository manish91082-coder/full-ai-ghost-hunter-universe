## 3. Immediate gaps found

1. A generic chain list does not expose flash-loan capability for every chain.
2. Protocol deployment does not automatically establish current flash-loan functionality.
3. DEX presence does not automatically establish profitable executable routes.
4. TVL does not equal executable liquidity at the required trade size.
5. Static addresses require network/version-specific verification.
6. L2/L3 discovery requires dedicated scaling/ecosystem coverage.
7. Non-EVM ecosystems require separate execution-model handling.
8. Retired, deprecated and testnet networks need explicit classification.
9. New networks require recurring discovery to prevent universe drift.
10. Source disagreement requires conflict records, not silent merging.
